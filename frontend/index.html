    How to read your result

    A fresh exact URL match in a public phishing feed takes priority. Otherwise, the local index uses the model share shown in the results, with the remaining share from security rules. It is not a probability or a safety guarantee. Low predicted risk does not prove a link is safe.

    The model was calibrated on historical data and may miss new attacks. Rules describe warning signals, not individual explanations of the model prediction.
